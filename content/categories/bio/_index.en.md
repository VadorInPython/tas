Hey there, 
I'm Szymon, and I decided to start something personal—specifically this blog, which serves as a blend of a diary, a guidebook, and a space for personal growth.
Building this place from scratch gives me an incredible sense of freedom, especially after all the turbulence I faced at the very beginning of this major life change.

But that's just the intro—generally speaking, I'll be sharing my activities here across several categories related to my adventure in Denmark. 
Starting with tips that might be helpful for anyone choosing a similar path, through showcases of projects that I really geeked out over, to posts about travels and cool spots worth visiting (both locally and abroad)—because, ultimately, there's more to life than just studying.

If something here catches your eye—stick around.
For now… just stay tuned.
