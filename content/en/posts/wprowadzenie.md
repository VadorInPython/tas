## Welcome and a short intro

Hi there!
Today I'll try to briefly summarize my story (without unnecessary details), tell you where the idea for this blog actually came from, and what you can expect here. If you've glanced at the tabs – you've already had a little sneak peek.

## Where did the blog idea come from?

Honestly? If someone asked me that straight up, I'd probably say: "I don't know." And only a moment later would something smarter pop into my head 😅.
And actually... it's no different here.

But to be completely honest:
I wanted to create a space here – my own four walls – where I can show what life and studies look like at one of the top European technical universities, in a country generally perceived as having the "best quality of life."

Besides, the world is rushing forward like crazy, and people who want to follow a similar path – especially students from Poland, of whom there are surprisingly many here – could have another, slightly more current, real, and practical source of information.
In short: I want this place to be helpful, honest, and grounded in reality – especially for student expats potentially heading to Danish universities.

## What will this blog be about?

As an enthusiast of everything related to the military – I couldn't pass up the option of organizing all articles into appropriate categories...

Okay – half-jokingly – because if someone sees this without context, they might think I'm some kind of drill sergeant. And I don't want it to look like that at all! Although... flexibility is my strong suit, so if there's ever a need to politely and smilingly steer someone onto the right track – I won't refuse 😉.

Getting back to the point:

### 🎓 Studies in Denmark

I'll be posting here:

- practical tips on admissions,
- handling formalities,
- how local bureaucracy works (spoiler: for an EU citizen – surprisingly well),
- and what daily student life looks like from the perspective of someone living here long-term.

### 💻 Tech & Projects

Something like my portfolio – projects that gave me the most fun, things I build after hours, and a bit more info on technologies that actually fall within my area of interest.
(Sounds like a nerd description... well okay, who am I trying to fool? 🤓)

From time to time, there will also be reports and photos from tech events related to my field of study.

### ✈️ On The Go

The truth is, every engineer needs a getaway (at least once in a while). So, finally, there will be access to a mix of what I do after hours, just so I don't end up with back pain in 10 years. Generally, you can expect:

- short reports from city breaks,
- places to recommend in Copenhagen,
- minor and major cultural differences,
- and everything that simply helps to take a breather after classes.

## In closing

I didn't expect the first post to turn out so long 🙃
I hope no one fell asleep reading it.

In future posts, concrete tips will appear regarding:
 - what the application process looks like
 - how to sort out your stay (both in terms of required documents and finding your own place)
 - what actually distinguishes the Danish education system
 - and later, of course, many other aspects related to these topics

See you soon!
And in the meantime – the perfect read for dark, winter evenings. ❄️📘
