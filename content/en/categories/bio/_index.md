Hi there, 
I'm Szymon, and I've decided to start something personal – specifically this blog, which will be a combination of a diary, a guide, and a space for growth.
Building this place from scratch gives me an incredible sense of freedom, especially after all the turbulence I faced at the very beginning of this life change.

However, that's just the intro – generally speaking, I'll be sharing my activities here across several categories related to my adventure in Denmark. Starting with any tips that might be helpful for those choosing a similar path, moving on to showcases of the projects that excited me the most, and eventually, posts about travels and interesting places worth visiting (both locally and abroad) will appear here too – after all, there is more to life than just studies.

If something here catches your interest – stay a while.
For now… just stay tuned.
