## Welcome & Short Intro

Hey there!
Today I’ll try to briefly summarize my story (skipping the unnecessary details), tell you where the idea for this blog came from, and what you can expect here. If you’ve already glanced at the menu tabs, you’ve had a little sneak peek.

## Why start a blog?

Honestly? If someone asked me point-blank, I’d probably say: "I don't know." Only after a moment would I come up with something smarter 😅.
And really... it's no different here.

But to be completely honest:
I wanted to create a space—my own four walls—where I can show what life and studies look like at one of the top European technical universities, in a country generally perceived as having the "best quality of life."

Besides, the world is rushing forward like crazy, and people who want to follow a similar path—especially international students—could use another source of up-to-date, real, and practical information.
In short: I want this place to be helpful, honest, and grounded in reality—especially for student expats potentially heading to Danish universities.

## What will this blog be about?

As an enthusiast of everything military-related, I couldn't resist the option of organizing all articles into proper categories...

Okay, jokes aside—because if someone sees this without context, they might think I’m some kind of Military Policeman. And I definitely don't want it to look like that! Although... flexibility is my strong suit, so if there's ever a need to politely and smilingly guide someone back on the right track—I won't say no 😉.

Back to the point:

### 🎓 Studies in Denmark

Here I’ll be posting:

- practical tips on recruitment,
- handling formalities,
- how local bureaucracy works (spoiler: for an EU citizen—surprisingly well),
- and what the daily life of a student looks like from the perspective of someone living here a bit longer.

### 💻 Tech & Projects

Sort of my portfolio—projects that gave me the most fun, things I build after hours, and a bit more info on technologies that actually fall within my area of interest.
(Sounds like a nerd description… well, okay, who am I trying to fool? 🤓)

From time to time, there will also be reports and photos from tech events related to my field.

### ✈️ On The Go

The truth is, every engineer needs a getaway (at least once in a while). So, finally, there will be a mix of what I do after hours so I don't end up with back pain in 10 years. In general, you can expect:

- short reports from city breaks,
- spots to recommend in Copenhagen,
- minor and major cultural differences,
- and everything that simply helps to catch a breath after classes.

## Wrapping up

I didn't expect the first post to turn out this long 🙃
I hope no one fell asleep reading it.

In the next posts, concrete tips will appear regarding:
 - what the university application process looks like,
 - how to sort out your stay (both in terms of documents and finding your own corner),
 - what actually distinguishes the Danish education system,
 - and later, of course, many other aspects related to these topics.

See you soon!
In the meantime—perfect reading for dark, winter evenings. ❄️📘
